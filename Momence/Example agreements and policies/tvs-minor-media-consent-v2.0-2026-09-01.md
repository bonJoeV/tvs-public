# MINOR MEDIA CONSENT AND GUARDIAN AUTHORIZATION

**The Vital Stretch - Twin Cities**

*Version 2.0 | Effective September 1, 2026*

---

## OPTIONAL CONSENT

This Media Consent is separate from the Minor Participant Agreement and every membership agreement. It is optional. Declining or revoking it does not affect the Minor's Services, eligibility, scheduling, pricing, or client standing.

## 1. Parties and Minor

**"Company"** means KJ Vital Holdings Inc., a Minnesota corporation doing business in Minnesota under the assumed name "The Vital Stretch."

**"Franchisor"** means The Vital Stretch Franchising LLC.

**"Minor"** means the participant under age 18 identified in the guardian-owned Company client account under which this consent is accepted.

**"Guardian"** means the parent or legal guardian signing this consent.

**"Media"** means photographs, video recordings, and audio recordings containing the Minor's image, likeness, voice, or first name.

## 2. Guardian Authority and Consent

The Guardian represents that the Guardian has legal authority to consent to the capture and use of Media involving the Minor.

By signing this form, the Guardian authorizes Company and Franchisor to capture Media of the Minor during an agreed photo, video, event, or Service session and to reproduce, edit, publish, display, and distribute that Media for The Vital Stretch marketing and promotional purposes.

Permitted channels include Company and Franchisor websites, social media accounts, email, digital advertisements, printed advertisements, studio displays, presentations, and other branded promotional materials.

## 3. Minor Assent

Guardian consent does not replace the Minor's assent. Staff will identify when Media is being captured and will ask whether the Minor agrees. Company will not photograph, record, interview, or continue capturing marketing Media if the Minor declines, appears unwilling, or asks to stop.

The Minor may decline a particular recording without revoking this consent for future occasions.

## 4. Limits

- Company and Franchisor may use the Minor's first name only. The Minor's last name will not be published.
- Media will not identify the Minor's school, home address, precise location, schedule, health information, medical conditions, intake responses, or session notes.
- This consent does not authorize testimonials or health-outcome statements by or about the Minor.
- Media may be edited for length, format, lighting, sound, captions, and layout, but not in a way that materially misrepresents the Minor.
- Company and Franchisor may provide Media to vendors acting on their behalf for production, hosting, distribution, and advertising. This consent does not authorize sale of the Minor's Media for an unrelated third party's independent use.
- This consent does not authorize marketing use of security camera footage.

## 5. Term and Revocation

This consent expires one year after the date it is signed unless the Guardian revokes it sooner. A new signed consent is required after expiration.

The Guardian may revoke consent at any time by telling Studio staff or by emailing twincities@thevitalstretch.com. The Minor may also ask Company to stop new use. Company will record the request and stop creating or approving new uses of the Minor's Media after processing it. Company will make reasonable efforts to remove the Minor's Media from digital materials it directly controls.

Revocation does not require recall of materials already printed, distributed, lawfully published, or republished by others before the revocation was processed.

## 6. Compensation and Ownership

Neither the Guardian nor the Minor will receive compensation or royalties unless a separate written agreement states otherwise. Company and Franchisor own the promotional materials they create, subject to the limits, expiration, and revocation rights in this consent.

## 7. Electronic Acceptance and Records

This consent is executed electronically. The Guardian's electronic signature has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature. Company will retain the version, date and time, signer identity, Minor identity, and account used and will provide the Guardian a copy that can be retained.

## 8. Acknowledgment

By signing and submitting this Media Consent, the Guardian confirms that:

- The Guardian has read and understands this consent;
- The Guardian has authority to consent for the Minor;
- The consent is given voluntarily;
- Media consent is not a condition of Services;
- The Minor retains the right to decline or stop any capture; and
- The Guardian understands the expiration and revocation process.

The Guardian accepts this Media Consent by applying an electronic signature in Company's booking and membership system. The Minor's identity and date of birth and the Guardian's identity, relationship, and contact information are held in the guardian-owned Company client account and incorporated into this consent by reference.
