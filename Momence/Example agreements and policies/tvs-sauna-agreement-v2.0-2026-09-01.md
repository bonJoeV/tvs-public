## 2. What the Sauna Is and Is Not

Company's saunas use infrared emitters and expose you to heat. The room may feel different from a traditional sauna, but heat-related illness can still occur. **Do not judge your heat exposure only by how warm the room feels.**

Sauna Use raises your body temperature and induces sweating. That is a physiological stress on your cardiovascular system, your fluid balance, and your ability to regulate temperature.

You understand and agree that:

- Sauna Use is a wellness and recreational amenity. It is not medical care, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or therapy, and it is not a substitute for any of them.
- Company staff are not physicians, nurses, physical therapists, or other licensed health care providers, and do not act in any such capacity in connection with the Sauna.
- **Company makes no representation, warranty, or claim that Sauna Use will produce any health benefit, including detoxification, weight loss, cardiovascular benefit, pain relief, recovery, immune benefit, or improvement of any condition.**
- Company staff do not monitor your vital signs, your fluid status, or your tolerance for heat. **You are responsible for monitoring yourself.**

## 3. Medical Screening, Contraindications, and Your Duty to Disclose

**Do not use the Sauna without first consulting your physician if any of the following applies to you:**

- Pregnancy, or a possibility of pregnancy
- Any heart condition, including coronary artery disease, arrhythmia, heart failure, prior heart attack, or a pacemaker, defibrillator, or other implanted cardiac device
- High or low blood pressure, or a history of fainting or orthostatic hypotension
- Any respiratory condition, including asthma or COPD
- Diabetes, peripheral neuropathy, or any condition that reduces sensation in your skin
- Kidney disease, liver disease, or any condition affecting fluid or electrolyte balance
- Seizure disorder or epilepsy
- Multiple sclerosis or any condition affecting temperature regulation
- Recent surgery, open wounds, skin infection, or acute injury or inflammation
- Fever, illness, or active infection
- Any implanted device, metal implant, joint replacement, or medical implant
- Use of medication that affects blood pressure, heart rate, fluid balance, sweating, sedation, or heat tolerance, including beta blockers, diuretics, antihistamines, stimulants, sedatives, antidepressants, and anticoagulants

**To the best of your knowledge, after having an opportunity to complete Company's sauna-specific screening, you represent that you have disclosed the conditions and medications above that apply to you. You agree to follow any use restrictions or clearance instructions from your physician and to notify Company before your next Sauna Use if any screening answer changes.**

**You must not use the Sauna if you are under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, recreational drugs, or any substance that impairs judgment, sensation, or temperature regulation.** Alcohol and heat together significantly increase the risk of fainting, dehydration, and cardiac events.

## 4. Assumption of Inherent Risks

Sauna Use involves risks that are inherent in heat exposure and cannot be eliminated regardless of the care taken. **You knowingly and voluntarily assume all such inherent risks**, including but not limited to:

- Dehydration and electrolyte imbalance
- Heat exhaustion and heat stroke
- Dizziness, lightheadedness, nausea, headache, confusion, and fainting, including on standing or on exiting
- Burns from contact with infrared panels, emitters, guards, benches, walls, doors, glass, or other hot surfaces
- Skin irritation, rash, or reaction
- Cardiovascular events, including elevated heart rate, arrhythmia, drop in blood pressure, heart attack, and stroke
- Aggravation, recurrence, or worsening of a pre-existing injury or condition
- Slips, trips, and falls on wet, sweat-covered, or heated surfaces, including inside the Sauna and in the surrounding area
- Overheating caused by staying in longer than you should
- Injury resulting from another occupant's conduct
- In rare cases, serious injury, permanent disability, or death

You understand that heat can impair your judgment about your own condition, which means you may not recognize that you are in trouble.

**By signing this Agreement you confirm that you have read this Section 4, that you understand these risks, and that you knowingly and voluntarily assume them.**

## 5. Rules of Use

You agree to follow these rules and any posted rules or staff instructions. They exist for your safety.

- **Hydrate before, during, and after Sauna Use.**
- **Limit each session to the shortest of the booked time, the posted Studio limit, the manufacturer's limit, or 45 minutes.** If this is your first Sauna Use at a Studio, do not exceed the shorter first-use limit posted by Company or stated by the manufacturer.
- **Exit immediately** if you feel dizzy, lightheaded, nauseated, short of breath, confused, unusually hot or cold, or unwell in any way, or if you experience chest pain, palpitations, headache, or tingling.
- **Stand up slowly** when exiting. Fainting most often happens on standing.
- **Cool down gradually** before showering, driving, or exerting yourself.
- **Do not use the Sauna alone in the building.** Sauna Use is permitted only during staffed studio hours.
- **Do not sleep in the Sauna.**
- **Use controls only as staff instructions and posted equipment directions permit.** Do not touch emitters, guards, or other hot surfaces.
- **Sit or lie on a towel.** Do not place skin directly on benches or walls.
- **Do not bring in** food, alcohol, glass, aerosols, flammable items, lotions, oils, essential oils, or electronics, and do not add any substance to the Sauna or its heating elements.
- **Do not apply lotions, oils, creams, self-tanner, or other topical products before or during Sauna Use.** Infrared energy is absorbed by the skin, and topical products can increase the risk of burns, blistering, and irritation.
- **Do not add water or any other substance to the Sauna, its panels, or its emitters.** These units are infrared and are not designed for water.
- **Wear appropriate attire.** Do not use the Sauna nude or in attire that violates posted studio rules.
- **Do not use the Sauna immediately after intense exercise or a heavy meal.**
- **Tell staff immediately** if you or another occupant appears unwell, or if any equipment, surface, door, or control appears damaged, unusually hot, or not working.
- **Do not block, prop, wedge, or obstruct the Sauna door.**
- Keep the Studio's method for calling staff accessible throughout Sauna Use. Exit immediately if the door, controls, timer, ventilation, or staff-call method does not function as expected.

Company may refuse, suspend, or end Sauna Use at any time for any lawful reason, including apparent impairment, apparent distress, or violation of these rules.
