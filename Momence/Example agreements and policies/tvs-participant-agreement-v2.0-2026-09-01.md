## 2. Description of Services

Services involve hands-on, practitioner-assisted stretching. A Practitioner will position, stabilize, and move parts of your body, and will apply manual force to lengthen muscles and mobilize joints. Services may also include movement screening, assessment of range of motion, and instruction in stretching and mobility techniques. Services are performed one-on-one, fully clothed, on a table or similar equipment.
