## Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how KJ Vital Holdings Inc., a Minnesota corporation doing business under the assumed name "The Vital Stretch," collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information. In this Policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean KJ Vital Holdings Inc.

This Policy covers the Company-operated Twin Cities studios listed in the Studio Locations Schedule then in effect, our local websites and lead forms, our booking and membership system, and our communications with you. The current Studio Locations Schedule is available at any Studio, at thevitalstretch.com/twin-cities/, and on request at twincities@thevitalstretch.com.

**Contact us about privacy at twincities@thevitalstretch.com.**

## HIPAA generally does not govern these Services

This matters, so we want to be direct about it.

We are a wellness and fitness business. In providing the Services described in this Policy, we are not currently acting as a covered entity or business associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. **HIPAA generally does not govern the information we collect for these Services, including information you give us about injuries, pain, medical conditions, or physical limitations. Other privacy, consumer-protection, security, and breach-notification laws may still apply.**

We protect that information and we treat it as confidential under this Policy. But the specific legal protections of HIPAA do not attach to it, and you should understand that before deciding what to share with us. If we provide you an invoice or receipt to submit to an insurer, health savings account, or flexible spending account, that does not make us a health care provider or bring us under HIPAA.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and mailing or billing address
- Emergency contact name and phone number
- Payment card or bank account information, collected and processed by our payment providers
- Health-related information you provide during intake and sessions, including injuries, surgeries, medical conditions, physical limitations, medications, pain and symptom reports, pregnancy, and physician clearance

- Booking, attendance, cancellation, and purchase history

- Cookies, pixels, and similar technologies, described below
- Security camera footage on our premises
- Records of your acceptance of our agreements, including date, time, version, and account used

**Information from others**

- Our booking and membership platform, payment processors, and other service providers
- Advertising and analytics platforms
- Referral sources, event partners, and franchise system channels

- To provide Services, including scheduling, session delivery, and building your stretch plan

## Health-related information

Information about your injuries, conditions, symptoms, and physical limitations is sensitive, and we handle it differently from other information.

- We use it to deliver Services safely and to build your stretch plan.
- Access is limited to the Practitioners and studio staff who need it to serve you, and to management for quality and safety purposes.
- **We do not sell it. We do not share it with advertising platforms, and we do not use it for advertising, ad targeting, audience building, or lookalike modeling.**
- We do not share it with your employer or insurer without your direction. We disclose it only to personnel and service providers who need it to provide, host, secure, or support Services; to professional advisors under confidentiality obligations; or as otherwise described under "When we share information" below.
- If you ask us to produce documentation for reimbursement, we share only what you direct us to share, with you.

## When we share information

We share personal information with:

**Service providers who work on our behalf**, including our booking and membership platform, payment processors, email and text messaging providers, scheduling and communication tools, analytics providers, IT and security vendors, and professional advisors. Their access depends on the service they provide. Our contracts and instructions limit their use of personal information to providing and supporting services for us, subject to applicable law.

**Advertising and analytics platforms**, including Meta and Google, for advertising measurement and audience purposes. This may include contact information such as a hashed email address or phone number and information about actions you took, such as booking an intro offer. It does not include health-related information. See "Your advertising choices" below.

**The Vital Stretch Franchising LLC and authorized franchise-system providers**, for brand, quality, reporting, system administration, and operational purposes. Health-related information is disclosed through franchise systems only where needed to provide or support Services and subject to role-based access; reporting uses aggregated or deidentified information where reasonably practicable.

**Others when required or permitted**, including to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, enforce our agreements, protect the rights, safety, or property of any person, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

**We do not sell your personal information for money.**

Some state privacy laws define "sale" and "targeted advertising" broadly enough to include sharing data with advertising platforms even when no money changes hands. If you want us to stop that sharing for you, email twincities@thevitalstretch.com and we will honor the request.

## Cookies, pixels, and tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including advertising pixels from Meta and Google, for site functionality, analytics, and advertising measurement.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You can also use the ad settings offered by Meta and Google to limit personalized advertising, and browser or extension based tracking controls. Blocking some cookies may affect how our site works.

Our reliable opt-out method is the request process described below. We also honor legally recognized browser-based opt-out preference signals where applicable law requires and our website receives the signal in a form it can process.

## Your communication choices

**Marketing email.** Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. You can also email us.

**Marketing text messages.** Reply STOP to any marketing text to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. **Consent to marketing texts is never a condition of purchasing or receiving Services.**

**Transactional messages.** Even after you opt out of marketing, we will still send messages necessary to your account, such as appointment confirmations and reminders, billing notices, and legally required notices. If you want to stop those as well, contact us, but understand it may affect our ability to serve you.

**Photos and video.** Our participant agreements do not grant marketing rights. Marketing use of your image, voice, name, or statements requires separate, optional written media consent. Security camera footage is used for security and operational purposes.

## Your advertising choices

If you do not want your information shared with advertising platforms for targeted advertising or measurement, email twincities@thevitalstretch.com with the subject line "Advertising opt out." We will remove you from advertising audiences we control and stop including you in future data shared with those platforms.

Health-related information is never included in advertising data regardless of your choice.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy
- Correct inaccurate personal information
- Delete personal information
- Opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, and certain profiling
- Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To make a request, email twincities@thevitalstretch.com. We will verify your identity before responding, which may require confirming information we already hold. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response, and you may contact the Minnesota Attorney General's Office.

Some information we must keep regardless of a deletion request, including records we need for legal, tax, accounting, safety, or dispute purposes, such as signed agreements and injury reports.

## Children

We do not provide Services to anyone under 13. Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 online.

We provide Services to participants aged 13 through 17 only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian under a separate Minor Participant Agreement. The guardian holds the account, and information about a minor is collected from and shared with the guardian rather than the minor. **We do not use a minor's image, likeness, voice, name, or statements in marketing without separate written consent from a parent or legal guardian.**

If you believe we have collected information from a child inappropriately, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including access controls, vendor selection and oversight, and limiting who can see health-related information. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify you as required by Minnesota law.

## Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this Policy and afterward where required for legal, tax, accounting, insurance, safety, and dispute purposes. Our written records retention schedule assigns retention periods by record category. Signed agreements, health-related intake records, session notes, and incident reports may be retained through applicable limitations and insurance periods. Records involving a minor may be retained longer because legal time periods can extend beyond the minor's eighteenth birthday.
