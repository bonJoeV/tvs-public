## 2. Your Membership

**Membership is not required to receive Services.** Services are available to anyone at Company's standard rates without a membership and without this Agreement. A membership provides discounted pricing on those same Services. You are not purchasing access to a facility; you are purchasing Services at a member rate.

**Two-month minimum commitment.** Your Initial Term consists of two monthly billing periods. After the Initial Term, your membership continues through successive Renewal Terms until you terminate under Section 7.

Your membership type, recurring dues amount, billing date, session credits or Services included, applicable taxes, and applicable fees are set out in your Purchase Summary.

This Agreement is dated as of the date you accept it, which Company records at acceptance.

## 3. Automatic Renewal and Continuous Service

**Your membership continues automatically until you terminate it.**

Your membership runs for the Initial Term, then renews automatically for successive Renewal Terms until you terminate it. Each renewal charges the recurring dues amount in your Purchase Summary.

**How to terminate:** you may terminate at any time using any of the methods in Section 7. Terminating is free and carries no penalty.

The amount charged may change if you purchase additional Services, products, session credits, sauna sessions, or retail items, or if applicable taxes, disclosed fees, or failed payment fees apply.

**Notices we will send you.** After you accept this Agreement, Company will send you a confirmation you can keep that includes your membership terms and how to terminate. Company will send you a written continuous service notice by mail or email at least once per calendar year describing your terms and how to terminate or manage your membership. Before any material change to your membership terms, Company will give you clear and conspicuous advance notice of the change and information on how to terminate.

**Online termination.** Company's member portal includes a clear and conspicuous termination election that lets you end your membership at any time, using a simple button or similar mechanism, and requiring only the information necessary to process the termination. The termination election remains available regardless of whether your account is current.

## 6. Five-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

**If you terminate within five Business Days of accepting this Agreement, Company will refund everything you have paid under it, within ten days of your notice.**

**Terminating within this period also releases you from the two-month minimum commitment in Section 2.** You will not be charged for the second month.

This is a Company policy. You do not need to give a reason, and Company will not ask for one.

## 7. Terminating Your Membership

**You may terminate at any time, at your sole discretion, for any reason.**

You may terminate by any of these methods:

- In person at any Studio
- By telephone to any Studio
- By mail to any Studio
- By email to twincities@thevitalstretch.com
- Through the termination election in our member portal
- By any other method through which you originally accepted this membership

You may send your notice to any Studio, not only the one where you enrolled. Notice need not take any particular form. It is enough that it indicates you intend to end your membership.

An in-person, telephone, email, portal, or online notice is effective when Company receives it. A properly addressed and prepaid mailed notice is effective when deposited in the mail. Company will send a confirmation stating the effective date, any final amount due, the last day of membership benefits, and the status of unused session credits.

**Company will not charge a termination fee, early termination penalty, or any other charge triggered by your decision to terminate.**

**When termination takes effect.** If you terminate during the Initial Term, your membership ends at the end of the Initial Term, and you remain responsible for dues for both monthly billing periods. Those dues are the minimum purchase obligation you accepted when you enrolled. They are not a termination fee, and the amount does not change because you terminated.

If you terminate during a Renewal Term, your membership ends at the end of that paid Renewal Term unless you request a later date. Company will not charge you for another Renewal Term after receiving your notice. Your membership benefits remain available through the effective date, subject to this Agreement.

**The five-business-day satisfaction guarantee in Section 6 overrides this Section.** If you terminate within that window, you owe nothing and are refunded in full.

**Company will not obstruct termination.** Suspension of Services, paused benefits, or a failed payment will never block your ability to submit a termination notice or reach the termination election.

Company may ask why you are terminating, but you are not required to answer. After receiving your notice, Company will not present a retention offer or delay termination unless you first give Company permission to discuss an alternative. Company may verify your identity and explain the consequences of termination.

**What survives termination.** You remain responsible for amounts that became due before the effective date of termination, including unpaid dues, failed payment fees, returned payment fees, retail and online purchases, and taxes. Termination may end eligibility for promotional pricing under Section 9.

## 11. Death, Permanent Disability, and Relocation

**Death or permanent disability.** If you die or become permanently disabled and cannot receive the Services you contracted for, you or your estate may terminate without penalty and receive a pro rata refund of unused prepaid amounts. Permanent disability means a condition that prevents you from using Company's Services. Company may request reasonable physician verification where allowed by law.

**Relocation.** If you move more than 25 miles from your Studio of enrollment and Company cannot transfer your membership to a comparable Studio within 25 miles of your new residence, you may terminate without penalty and receive a pro rata refund of unused prepaid amounts.

## 12. Membership Freeze

Company may allow eligible members to temporarily pause a membership instead of terminating. A freeze is not a termination and does not change your rights under Section 7.

Freezes are available for eligible membership types selected by Company, including monthly stretch and sauna memberships. Eligible reasons include medical condition or illness, injury, family emergency, and excessive unused session credits.

Maximum freeze duration is 3 months. Each membership may be frozen a maximum of 3 times.

Company does not currently charge a freeze fee. Company reserves the right to charge a one-time freeze processing fee of up to $15 in the future. Any such fee will be clearly disclosed before the freeze is approved and will not be charged unless you agree to proceed after that disclosure. Company may waive it at its discretion.

Freeze requests are subject to review and approval, and Company may request reasonable supporting information. Company may allow exceptions to this policy at its discretion.

Every approved freeze will be confirmed in writing and state its start date, end date, billing treatment, benefit access, session-credit treatment, promotional-rate treatment, and resumption date. When the freeze ends, the membership resumes automatically on those written terms unless you have terminated it.

You remain responsible for amounts owed before, during, or after the freeze. A freeze does not waive amounts already owed.

## 14. Failed Payments

If a recurring payment is declined, returned, or otherwise unsuccessful, Company may re-run the payment on its retry schedule.

Current failed payment fee schedule:

- First re-run attempt: no fee
- Second re-run attempt: $5
- Third re-run attempt: $10

Failed-payment fees for one scheduled payment will not exceed $15 in total. Company will not add a separate returned-payment or service fee for the same failed payment unless the additional fee is disclosed in advance and permitted by law.

If a payment fails, Company may pause benefits, session usage, sauna usage, future bookings, and account access until the account is brought current. Existing bookings may be canceled or restricted. A member with an unresolved failed payment is not in good standing.

This does not affect your ability to terminate under Section 7.
