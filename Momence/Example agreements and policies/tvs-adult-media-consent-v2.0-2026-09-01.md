## OPTIONAL CONSENT

This Media Consent is separate from every membership, participant, and sauna agreement. It is optional. Declining or revoking it does not affect Services, eligibility, scheduling, pricing, or client standing.

## 1. Parties

**"Company"** means KJ Vital Holdings Inc., a Minnesota corporation doing business in Minnesota under the assumed name "The Vital Stretch."

**"Franchisor"** means The Vital Stretch Franchising LLC.

**"Media"** means photographs, video recordings, audio recordings, and the image, likeness, voice, first name, and statements captured in them.

## 2. Consent Granted

By signing this form, you authorize Company and Franchisor to capture Media of you during an agreed photo, video, interview, event, or Service session and to reproduce, edit, publish, display, and distribute that Media for The Vital Stretch marketing and promotional purposes.

Permitted channels include Company and Franchisor websites, social media accounts, email, digital advertisements, printed advertisements, studio displays, presentations, and other branded promotional materials.

Company will identify when marketing Media is being captured. This consent does not authorize hidden recording or marketing use of security camera footage.

## 3. Limits

- Company and Franchisor may use your first name but will not publish your last name without additional written consent.
- Health information, medical conditions, intake responses, and session notes are not included in Media without separate, specific written consent.
- A testimonial or outcome statement may be used only if it reflects your honest experience and is not materially altered or presented as a guaranteed or typical result without appropriate context.
- Media may be edited for length, format, lighting, sound, captions, and layout, but not in a way that materially misrepresents you or your statements.
- Company and Franchisor may provide Media to vendors acting on their behalf for production, hosting, distribution, and advertising. This consent does not authorize sale of your Media for an unrelated third party's independent use.

## 4. Term and Revocation

This consent begins when you sign and continues until you revoke it.

You may revoke consent at any time by telling Studio staff or by emailing twincities@thevitalstretch.com. Company will record the revocation and stop creating or approving new uses of your Media after processing it. Company will make reasonable efforts to remove your Media from digital materials it directly controls.

Revocation does not require recall of materials already printed, distributed, lawfully published, or republished by others before the revocation was processed.

## 5. Compensation and Ownership

You will not receive compensation or royalties unless a separate written agreement states otherwise. Company and Franchisor own the promotional materials they create, subject to the limits and revocation rights in this consent.

## 6. Electronic Acceptance and Records

This consent is executed electronically. Your electronic signature has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature. Company will retain the version, date and time, signer identity, and account used and will provide you a copy you can retain.

By signing and submitting this Media Consent, you confirm that:

- You are at least 18 years old;
- You have read and understand this consent;
- You are signing voluntarily;
- Media consent is not a condition of Services; and
