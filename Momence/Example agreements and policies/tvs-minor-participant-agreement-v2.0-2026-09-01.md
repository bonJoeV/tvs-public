## 3. Description of Services and Consent to Physical Contact

Services involve hands-on, practitioner-assisted stretching. A Practitioner will position, stabilize, and move parts of the Minor's body, and will apply manual force to lengthen muscles and mobilize joints. Sessions are performed one-on-one, fully clothed, on a table or similar equipment.

**You consent, on the Minor's behalf, to this physical contact as described.**

## 4. Supervision and Safeguarding

These requirements apply to every Service provided to a Minor. They exist to protect the Minor.

- **A parent or legal guardian must remain on the premises for the entire duration of every session.** Company will not begin or continue a session for a Minor if no parent or guardian is present on site.
- **Sessions with a Minor are conducted in an open or visible area of the Studio, not behind a closed or locked door.** The Guardian may observe any session in full at any time and does not need to ask permission or give a reason.
- The Minor may stop, pause, or decline any part of a session at any time, for any reason, without explanation.
- Before each session begins, the Practitioner will confirm that the Minor agrees to participate. The Guardian's consent does not replace the Minor's assent, and Company will not begin or continue a session if the Minor does not wish to participate.
- The Guardian or the Minor may request a different Practitioner at any time, without giving a reason and without affecting scheduling or pricing.
- Practitioners will not communicate with the Minor privately outside of scheduled sessions. All scheduling, follow-up, session summaries, and other communication go to the Guardian.

If Company cannot meet these conditions for a given appointment, Company will reschedule rather than proceed.
